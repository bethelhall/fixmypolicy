


Should Be Allowed But Explicitly Denied (cont.) - These request SHOULD have been explicitly ALLOWEd in order for them not being blocked from access at all times; however they were instead explicitly DEnY'D due tO some other statement(or combination) which is more specific than this one.. This means that even though these requests would normally be permitted under normal circumstances based on their actions alone,they will now always be forbidden because another rule has taken precedence over it. So make sure you check ALL possible combinations when writing policies!
